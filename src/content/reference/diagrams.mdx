## How it's organized

Live diagrams are grouped by the Part where they were first built, in reading order, each linking back to every chapter that uses it — several diagrams (D1, D72, D81, D90) appear on more than one page. One representative diagram per Part is reproduced below, inline, exactly as it renders on its host chapter — the same component, the same props, so nothing here can drift from the page it's quoting. The rest of each Part's diagrams are listed by D-number and link; the backlog table after that covers only the 16 unbuilt diagrams from the original 39-diagram scope, not the remaining 51 diagrams in Appendix A's full 90-diagram inventory that were never part of v1 to begin with — those live in the build-planning backlog, not here.
