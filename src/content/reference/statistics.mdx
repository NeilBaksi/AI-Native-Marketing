## How it's organized

Stats are grouped by Part, in the order their host chapters appear, not by size or topic — this is a lookup list, not a ranking. Within a Part, related stats are clustered into rows of 2–4 (a `<Scorecard>` each) rather than stacked one per line, so a Part's numbers can be scanned in one pass instead of scrolled past one at a time. A row's mono label names the shared theme; it isn't a fifth data point.
