## The foundational source

- Mohanbir Sawhney, "Don't Just Add AI to Marketing. Rebuild Marketing for AI," *MIT Sloan Management Review*, 2026 — the article the whole I-MOS framework operationalizes, cited across nearly every session's endnotes.

## Strategy, positioning, and jobs-to-be-done

- Clayton M. Christensen, Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, and David S. Duncan, "Know Your Customers' 'Jobs to Be Done,'" *Harvard Business Review*, September 2016.
- Clayton M. Christensen, Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, and David S. Duncan, *Competing Against Luck*, HarperBusiness, 2016 — the full JTBD theoretical treatment behind [Focus](/workflows/focus).
- Theodore Levitt, "Marketing Myopia," *Harvard Business Review*, 1960 — the "quarter-inch drill" precursor to JTBD.
- Philip Kotler, *Marketing Management*, 1967 — the original source of the STP framework [Focus](/workflows/focus) builds on.
- Philip Kotler and Kevin Lane Keller, *Marketing Management*, 15th ed., Pearson, 2016.
- Wendell R. Smith, "Product Differentiation and Market Segmentation as Alternative Marketing Strategies," *Journal of Marketing*, July 1956.
- Jim Stengel, Cait Lamberton, and Ken Favaro, "How Brand Building and Performance Marketing Can Work Together," *Harvard Business Review*, May–June 2023.
- Christine Moorman, "When AI Investments Pay Off in Marketing," *MIT Sloan Management Review*, April 2024.

## Discovery, search, and answer engines

- Rand Fishkin and SparkToro (with Datos), "2024 Zero-Click Search Study," SparkToro.com, July 2024 — the ~900M-query analysis behind [Attract](/workflows/attract)'s zero-click figures.
- Scott Brinker and Frans Riemersma, chiefmartec.com martech landscape research — cited across the course material at two different tool counts; see [Statistics](/reference/statistics) for the discrepancy.

## Behavioral economics

- Richard H. Thaler and Cass R. Sunstein, *Nudge: Improving Decisions About Health, Wealth, and Happiness*, 2008 — the behavioral-economics treatment of stated versus revealed preference behind [Sense](/workflows/sense)'s skepticism of what customers say they want.

## Governance and regulation — primary sources

- UK Information Commissioner's Office, Enforcement Notice against Honda Motor Europe Limited, 2022 (ico.org.uk) — the regulatory document underlying [The Problem](/foundations/the-problem)'s Honda example, distinct from the news coverage linked above.
- U.S. Federal Trade Commission, "FTC Returns More Than $7.8 Million to Consumers Who Were Deceived by BetterHelp's Privacy Practices," FTC.gov, March 2024 — the source for [Focus](/workflows/focus)'s BetterHelp trust-boundary example.
- Airbnb, "Measuring Discrimination on the Airbnb Platform," June 2020, and "2024 Project Lighthouse Update," December 2024 — the source for [The Seven Workflows](/spine/seven-workflows)'s Project Lighthouse example.

## Interoperability protocols

- Anthropic, "Model Context Protocol (MCP): Connecting AI Models to the World," November 2024 — the open standard for connecting agents to tools, named in the course material behind [Build](/workflows/build)'s agentic-services architecture, though not yet detailed in the live chapter itself.
- Google, "Agent2Agent (A2A) Protocol," Google GitHub Repository, 2025 — the agent-to-agent interoperability standard named alongside MCP.
