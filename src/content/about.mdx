This is an independent study aid built around the concepts taught in MKTG 468: AI-Native Marketing at the Kellogg School of Management — a working handbook for the seven-workflow, four-layer operating system the course calls I-MOS, written to be read front-to-back or dropped into mid-chapter from a search.

It's built by a student who took the course, as a personal study aid — not an official course product, not endorsed by Kellogg or the instructor, and not a substitute for taking the class. Where the site draws on real course material, it says so; where a number or example is course-sourced rather than independently verified, or synthetic rather than real, [the provenance is marked](/reference/statistics), not left ambiguous.
