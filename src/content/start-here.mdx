<Section index="02" kicker="Choose one" title="Three reading paths">

**[Front to back](/foundations/the-problem).** The handbook is written as a sequence, each part building on the last. Start at The Problem and keep going.

**[By workflow](/spine/seven-workflows).** If you already know roughly what you're looking for, whether that's how discovery works in an AI-mediated search era or how journeys get orchestrated, start at The Seven Workflows and jump to the one you need. Prerequisite chips at the top of each page tell you what to read first if you're missing context.

**[By case](/cases).** If you learn better from a worked example than from a framework, start with a case study and follow its cross-links back into the workflow pages it draws on.

</Section>
