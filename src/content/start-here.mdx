<Section index="02" kicker="Choose one" title="Three reading paths">

**[Front to back](/foundations/the-problem).** The handbook is written as a sequence — each part builds on the last. Start at The Problem and keep going; many chapters close with a "Where this leads" pointer to what to read next.

**[By workflow](/spine/seven-workflows).** If you already know roughly what you're looking for — how discovery works in an AI-mediated search era, say, or how journeys get orchestrated — start at The Seven Workflows and jump to the one you need. Prerequisite chips at the top of each page tell you what to read first if you're missing context.

**[By case](/cases).** If you learn better from a worked example than from a framework, start with a case study and follow its cross-links back into the workflow pages it draws on.

</Section>

<Section index="04" kicker="No quiz" title="What 'done reading' looks like">

There's no test at the end. You'll know the material landed when you can look at a real marketing failure — your own organization's or someone else's — and name which of the seven workflows broke, and why. That's the actual skill this handbook is teaching.
