This toolkit is 15 fill-in artifacts. Each one operationalizes a concept Parts I–III already taught in full, from the ICP formula to the four autonomy tiers. None of them re-explain the concept. Instead, each hands a team the artifact itself — something to carry into a meeting or load into a content pipeline.

<Section index="01" kicker="Read order, not a random list" title="How it's organized">

The 15 templates are grouped by where they sit in the I-MOS workflow, in the order a team would actually reach for them. Strategy and positioning come first. That's what Focus and Design decide. Orchestration and journey design come next, covering how it runs, and governance and risk follow, covering what stops it from running badly. Measurement comes next, proving the effort worked. Last is a single roadmap for sequencing the rollout itself, and within a group, order matters. A messaging pillar spec built before the positioning statement it's supposed to draw from is usually redone within a month.

</Section>

<Section index="02" kicker="Grouped by workflow" title="The toolkit">

### Strategy & positioning

- **[Positioning Statement](/toolkit/positioning-statement)** — the seven-slot template (For, Who, Is, That, Because, Unlike, Our difference is) that turns [Focus's positioning logic](/workflows/focus) into a claim precise enough for an AI system to reuse.
- **[Messaging Pillar Spec](/toolkit/messaging-pillar-spec)** — three or four fixed themes, each with support points, proof cues, and a "do not say" line naming the overclaim that theme invites.
- **[ICP Scoring Rubric](/toolkit/icp-scoring-rubric)** — a weighted, per-account worksheet that operationalizes [Focus's Fit × Readiness − Friction formula](/workflows/focus) into a documented, auditable score.
- **[Decision-Narrative Persona](/toolkit/decision-narrative-persona)** — the fillable version of Focus's five-field persona (JTBD, Anxiety, Proof, Channel, Tone), built as a primary/secondary/negative trio.
- **[Brand DNA Spec](/toolkit/brand-dna-spec)** — the machine-readable version of [Design's five Brand DNA components](/workflows/design), with a worked example and an append-only violation-words list.

### Orchestration & journey design

- **[Agent Design Framework](/toolkit/agent-design-framework)** — a ten-component spec table for designing any AI agent's role, inputs, routing logic, and kill-switch, plus an eleventh row naming what the agent must never do.
- **[Journey-State Model](/toolkit/journey-state-model)** — a state-based journey template with entry/exit criteria, a dwell minimum, the next-best-action logic, and a cooldown rule to stop a customer flapping between states.

### Governance & risk

- **[Governance Table](/toolkit/governance-table)** — a per-workflow table of highest-risk failure, control, and measurable kill-switch, plus a required "what was left out and why" section.
- **[AI Hallucination Guardrails](/toolkit/ai-hallucination-guardrails)** — a pre-submission checklist and source-of-truth hierarchy for catching a fabricated claim before it reaches a customer-facing asset.
- **[Economics of Error Worksheet](/toolkit/economics-of-error-worksheet)** — the fillable version of [the cost-of-error vs. value-of-speed calculation](/spine/economics-of-error) that derives which of the four autonomy tiers a given decision belongs in.

### Measurement & learning

- **[Experiment Design Checklist](/toolkit/experiment-design-checklist)** — seven required fields (a falsifiable hypothesis, what changes, primary and guardrail metrics, design, duration, and the decision the result enables) for any test before it launches.
- **[Measurement Humility Worksheet](/toolkit/measurement-humility-worksheet)** — a two-column "what the data shows vs. what it proves" template for catching a correlation dressed up as a causal result.
- **[ROMI Formulas](/toolkit/romi-formulas)** — the contribution-based return-on-marketing-investment formula set, with the discipline of stating and sensitivity-testing the COGS assumption behind it.
- **[Data Readiness Audit](/toolkit/data-readiness-audit)** — a field-by-field worksheet for what to check, what was found, and how to handle it before any segmentation or scoring runs on a dataset.

### Implementation

- **[30-60-90 Roadmap](/toolkit/30-60-90-roadmap)** — a five-workstream rollout plan (instrument, pilot, measure, govern, org shift) sequenced so nothing gets measured before it's instrumented, or scaled before it's governed.
