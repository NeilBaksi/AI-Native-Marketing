Misjudge the stage, misjudge the fix. An organization can pour effort into faster orchestration while its actual weak point is governance running nowhere near the same speed. Every organization lands somewhere on a four-stage maturity model, and it's worth being direct about the punchline before walking through the stages:

<Callout kind="key" title="No company is at Stage 4">
Full I-MOS is aspirational. It's the name for an endpoint nobody has actually built yet. If a stage below feels uncomfortably familiar, that's the point of a diagnostic, not a failing grade.
</Callout>

Placing an organization on this ladder means reading it one workflow family at a time — the ladder is only as high as the weakest family, not the average of all four.

An organization's stage on the ladder above is set by the family sitting furthest back — a system that orchestrates fast but governs slow is a Stage 3 system, not a Stage 4 one, no matter how far ahead the other three families are.
