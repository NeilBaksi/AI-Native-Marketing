<Section index="02" kicker="Not a review step" title="Envelope, not gate">

Every diagram of the seven workflows in this handbook draws Govern as a band running beneath all seven, not as an eighth box at the end of the sequence — and that placement is the whole argument. A gate is something work passes through once, at the end, before it ships. An envelope is a property every workflow holds continuously, while it's running, not after.

Blocking and Advisory aren't two stops on a pipeline — they're the two answers to one test, applied per check, with Continuous logging running underneath both regardless of which answer a given check gets.
