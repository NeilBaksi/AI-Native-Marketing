<Section index="02" kicker="Not a review step" title="Envelope, not gate">

Every diagram of the seven workflows in this handbook draws Govern as a band running beneath all seven, not as an eighth box tacked onto the end of the sequence like one more step to clear. A gate is something work passes through once, at the end, before it ships. An envelope is a property every workflow holds continuously, while it's running, not after.

Blocking and Advisory answer one test, not two stops on a pipeline. Two answers, one test. Continuous logging runs underneath both, applied per check, regardless of which answer a given check gets.
