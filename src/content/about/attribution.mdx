This handbook draws on the concepts, frameworks, and terminology taught in **MKTG 468: AI-Native Marketing** at the Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, taught by Prof. Mohanbir Sawhney. Framework names and terminology used throughout this site — including "I-MOS," "the Insight Gap," and "the Economics of Error" — originate with that course and are credited to it here and on first substantive use per page.

Every page on this site is written in original prose by the site's author, a student in that course, as an independent study aid. Quoted excerpts are used sparingly, attributed inline, and never used to carry the substance of a section. No exhibit, table, or case narrative from the course is reproduced verbatim; diagrams are rebuilt as original visual work communicating the same underlying idea.

<Callout kind="key" title="Independent, unaffiliated study aid">
This site is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by** Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, or Prof. Sawhney.
</Callout>

Real-company case studies on this site are independently sourced from public filings, press releases, and reputable press — the course material is used only to identify which cases matter and what they illustrate, never as the source of a published fact about a real company.
