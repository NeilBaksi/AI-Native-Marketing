Seven full cases, one company each, chosen because each one makes a specific I-MOS failure or success visible in a way a framework description alone can't. Three built the loop well — L'Oréal, Coca-Cola's Fizzion build, National Australia Bank, and Peloton all show a workflow done right, at real scale. Two show what breaks when a workflow runs without the constraint it needed — OpenAI's sycophancy rollback and the governance roll-call. Yelp sits in between: a company mid-decision, watching the ground move under a business model that assumed a customer would always arrive at a destination.
